# BRN successor research contract

Revision 1, 2026-10-04 (Pacific/Auckland). Authority: the owner's current
Contract-Governed Long-Horizon Workstream request. This scoped contract governs
the successor investigation, not other projects or shared behavioral policy.
Read [state](BRN_SUCCESSOR_STATE.md), then the
[research record](../research/brn/BRN_SUCCESSOR_RESEARCH.md) to resume.

## Objective, controls and scope

Determine the strongest BRN architecture supported by controlled evidence, starting
from actual BRN-3 at `12e1398`. No successor topology or requirement to replace
BRN-3 is predetermined. The three references are unchanged NNUE, original BRN-3
(including its quarter-residual play calibration), and the current BRN champion.
Keep original weights, model formats, training stores and reproducible source
provenance. Implementation optimizations must remain distinguishable from learned
architecture changes. Incompatible learned topology gets an experimental identity;
BRN-4 is reserved for a justified integrated successor, not the first prototype.

Scope: neural evaluation/training, BRN runtime, controlled experiments, required
architecture configuration, persistence and comparison. Preserve NNUE behavior,
optimizer, score mapping and checkpoint compatibility. No unrelated board, move
generation, search, GUI or platform redesign. No push, deployment, normal Best-store
write, destructive Git operation or silent checkpoint migration is authorized.

Retain fixed material and zero residual initialization as controls. No handcrafted
positional/tactical features. The current request explicitly opens investigation
of non-placement state: older completed programmes' geometry-only restriction
remains binding for the original BRN-3, but is not a veto on isolated successor
experiments with justified rule-state inputs. Such inputs require evidence of
relevance, leakage-aware data grouping and explicit new model identity. Clocks and
history handled by rules/search are not automatically neural features.

## Evidence and selection

Repository code establishes implementation facts; hypotheses do not. Measure a
suspected limitation before prescribing its cure. Derive credible alternatives
from BRN's piece-local representation and cost model; research primary literature
when useful. Compare affordable materially different families. Negative results,
failed runs and superseded decisions remain evidence. Novelty is not a metric.

Each experiment records question, predicted discriminant, candidate identity,
source and split hashes, seed, exposure, initialization/resume lineage, optimizer,
calibration, model/cost budget, actual checks, results, disposition and next action.
Change as few variables as possible. Distinguish equal exposure, parameter count,
training compute and inference/search compute. Position grouping prevents equivalent
inputs crossing partitions. No game-disjoint claim without game identities. Old
opened test sets are historical evidence; selection uses validation only and final
confirmation must use a newly frozen source range/opening set.

Use compile, independent forward/gradient checks, tiny overfit, codec/resume checks,
bounded training and latency first. Only survivors enter larger samples/repeated
seeds, search/qsearch, games and integration. Production search currently uses
static leaves; qsearch is a separate research seam. Do not mistake zero production
qnodes for qsearch evidence. Loss and playing strength are distinct. Preserve raw
training and quarter-residual play results separately, and treat calibration as an
experimental variable rather than granting candidates an invisible advantage.

A runtime-only promotion needs the same mathematical function, declared numerical
bound (at most the existing 1e-4 pawn bound), edge-case/cache-isolation checks,
and repeat warmed measured benefit with no material search regression. Compare
integer scores and search decisions; investigate any changes. Capture, promotion,
en-passant, castling, STM, parent/child/sibling and distant traversals are relevant.

An architectural micro-screen may advance at >=2% relative held-out loss improvement
or a credible Pareto cost improvement; this is an admission rule, not promotion.
An architectural promotion requires independent seed/range replication, nontrivial
learning versus material, numerical/persistence correctness, realistic inference
and completed controlled search, followed by paired equal-time games on fresh
openings. Freeze question-specific sample, uncertainty and noninferiority/improvement
thresholds before those runs. Do not invent a winner when candidates trade quality
for cost. Final claims must match actual uncertainty; no NNUE superiority claim
from loss or mismatched controls. Recompare accumulated changes to original BRN-3.

## Autonomy and compute

Autonomously reorder/split/combine hypotheses, build isolated candidates, generalize
only necessary infrastructure, reject/defer/promote with evidence, commit coherent
attributable work, and continue. The dependency queue is dynamic. No permission
request merely because a reversible technical choice exists. Preserve independent
later-work attribution while historical version finalization is blocked.

Default early training uses 4k/16k examples and one to eight epochs, then at most
131072 per range where justified. One empirical process at a time; use available
CPU/RAM responsibly and fresh ignored output directories. Individual early runs
have a five-minute bound. Larger training/matches require a recorded information
need, cheaper-gate evidence, explicit local resource/time bound and termination
conditions. They are experiments, not routine validation. Do not duplicate an
active user campaign, run an 8M production campaign, or infer cloud-spend authority.
If required evidence exceeds a responsible local envelope, record the exact
unresolved decision; continue independent admissible work before escalating.

## Completion and stop conditions

Complete only when every admitted material question is supportedly retained,
rejected, immaterial, or explicitly deferred/blocked with proportionate evidence;
accepted mechanisms pass appropriate checks; dependencies and interactions have
been reassessed; integrated ablations establish useful mechanisms and remove
redundancy; and final BRN-3/champion/NNUE comparisons report capacity, training,
held-out quality, runtime/search and actual playing evidence. Any justified
successor must be coherently integrated into ordinary network workflow with new
identity and protected original stores. A finding that BRN-3 remains best within
the studied envelope is admissible, but unfinished promising experiments are not
completion. Fresh sessions must recover facts, decisions, blockers and next work
from repository records. Turn completion is not owner acceptance or deployment.

Stop affected work for unsafe ownership, unresolved authority conflict, missing
required data/hardware/credentials, necessary unavailable validation, disproportional
required compute, irreversible action without authority, scientifically unsound
premises, or a genuinely value-based architecture decision. Failed candidates are
not blockers. Do not expand scope or waive human gates to manufacture closure.

Authority order: current user and applicable higher instructions; this scoped
contract; verified experimental evidence and recorded decisions; operational state
and hypotheses. Evidence can falsify hypotheses and trigger a contract revision,
but cannot silently waive invariants. Revision rationale must identify affected
comparisons. Historical contracts remain evidence within their completed scope.
