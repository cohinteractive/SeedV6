Current disposition: pair tables are the principal practical candidate after Z01, but their own-Gen0 learning and native-BRN advantage remain inconclusive and their point score loses to cheap material. Both pair curves plateau by300s despite~50M exposures at600s; triple tables retain better raw loss with mixed late gains. Nested-data and longer-budget games remain pending; C02 is a prospective inference-equivalent compilation follow-up. Historical steps below are retained.

Initial recorded status: feasibility implementation; protocol frozen before training,2026-10-06.
Question: can exact learned joint square-state functions offer a quality/cost
alternative to continuous relation embeddings and MLP/product readouts?

N-tuple value functions have primary precedent in
[Lucas's Othello study](https://repository.essex.ac.uk/3820/) and
[Jaskowski's systematic short-tuple study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.1509).
These motivate a mechanism and a two-square ablation; their Othello performance
does not establish chess strength or Java CPU cost. This experiment learns
supervised chess residuals with the existing CP/WDL targets, not TD Othello values.

## Frozen initial design

Each square has13 states:empty,or one of12 ownership/type categories. Eight shapes
are translated over every valid anchor:horizontal3,vertical3,both diagonal3,
two orientations of a unit right triangle,and right triangles of leg2 and3.
There are327 anchored templates. Separate mover/opponent canonical perspectives
use the existing color/rank transformation. Each role/template/state owns an exact
scalar table entry. A training-only role/shape/state entry provides translation
sharing and folds into the absolute entry at inference. No hashing or collisions.
Residual=bias+sum(active absolute+shared entries)/sqrt(654). Add the unchanged
fixed material prior. All learned weights start at zero;Gen0 is material-only.

Order3 has1436839 inference and1471991 training parameters. The causal order2
ablation ignores the third cell of the SAME327 templates:110527 inference and
113231 training parameters. This deliberately preserves feature multiplicity and
geometry;duplicate pairs and unequal table capacity are disclosed limitations.
It tests whether using a third categorical cell justifies its memory/sparsity cost,
not a parameter-matched universal comparison of function order.

Inference caches canonical square codes,tuple addresses and four role/perspective
sums. Changed placements update only incident templates;STM changes select cached
sums. Rule-state/hash values do not enter the learned function. Full recomputation,
gradient finite differences,tiny fit,color/rank symmetry,information isolation,
exact optimizer resume/CRC and legal/special transition parity gate real training.
Report active-state coverage on validation without fitting to its labels.

Same data02 population/order/first4096 selection,CE,masked Adam.003,batch128,
seeds71/97,quarter residual play. Initial four runs:orders2/3 x16384 x4epochs;
<=300s and2GiB heap each. No random weight initialization;seeds change order only.
At most three additional justified recipes remain within the contract's four-recipe
limit. If weak,inspect learning curves,coverage and conditioning before disposition.
Cost opportunity can admit serious131072x8 even without best validation loss.
No sealed test labels and no production selector changes.
