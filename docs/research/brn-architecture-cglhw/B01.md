Current disposition: linear readout is the secondary practical candidate after Z01. It shows clear own-Gen0 learning but loses to cheap material; longer-budget gains are mixed and raw loss remains above BRN in both ranges. Nested-data and longer-budget games remain pending. Energy/rank16 expansion remains deferred. Historical steps below are retained.

Initial recorded status: admitted for implementation after E001 short-control interpretation.
All controls learned statically,128roots/96pilot games completed; no strength
ranking is established. B01 may test its representation hypothesis without treating
raw loss as an engine win. No candidate result is claimed. This is one mechanism
family, not a combined novelty model.

The existing BRN3 local representation is retained: two perspectives, width8
learned absolute+relative relations, local ReLU, typed pooling. Let x be its192
pooled relational coordinates. Replace the192->32ReLU->1 readout with

`R(x) = b + w.x + sum_r alpha_r * (u_r.x) * (v_r.x)`.

This is a low-rank bilinear energy over relation summaries, including learned
relation-conjunctions without a generic hidden activation/readout. A signed alpha
permits both favourable and unfavourable motifs. Linear bypass is an explicitly
measured constituent, not hidden material; material remains fixed and separate.
No combinatorial enumeration of relation pairs. Rank8 initially; rank16 and a
linear-only ablation are admissible only to answer capacity/causality questions.
All local encoders start from exactly the same seed/initialization as the BRN
control. Zero alpha/linear/bias gives exact material Gen0; factor vectors get
independent bounded random initialization so gradients can begin. Masked Adam .003,
batch128, same CE/targets/shuffles, quarter-residual search initially. Changed
learning rate may be tested if gradient/conditioning evidence justifies it.

Hypothesis: explicit products improve motif representation/data efficiency or
reduce readout cost enough to improve practical strength under the same budget.
Counterhypotheses: low rank loses necessary local gating; unbounded products amplify
score volatility; most cost resides in relation extraction so cheaper readout is
immaterial; apparent benefits are merely the linear bypass or calibration.

Required correctness: independently evaluate explicit matrix products; finite
differences for factors/alpha/linear head AND relation/local parameters with a
nonzero head; compare retained encoder/control gradients against original BRN;
tiny overfit including material-reversing labels; color/rank/STM equivalence,
forbidden-input invariance; codec rejection and exact continuation; float-folded
versus double numerical budget1e-4pawn; cache transition agreement where used.

First gate after correctness: matched16kx4 seeds71/97 on E001data02, all results
recorded. At least2% relative validation improvement, or a demonstrated credible
cost Pareto opportunity, admits131kx8; a close result can receive one justified
capacity/conditioning probe. Do not reject the family for an implementation bug
or a single poorly conditioned configuration. Compare exposure and real trainer
time, acknowledging generic versus optimized kernels until equivalently measured.
Final decisions require games, repeat seeds/ranges and loss/search-scale inspection.

External rationale: [Blondel et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/blondel16.html)
connect polynomial interactions to low-rank structure. Applying that mechanism
to pooled chess relations is this programme's hypothesis, not a published chess
strength result. Earlier successor factorization compressed edge messages while
retaining the MLP; it did not test this readout replacement.
