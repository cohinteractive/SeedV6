# R037 shared neural geometry and occupancy-dependent messages

Prospective architectural investigation after R036. The small played corpus is
memorized (trainMSE .00145-.00164,development .0366-.0395) and its mixture fails
transfer/old-panel guards. Do not tune that mixture or infer that all new data is
useless. R028 uses independent target-type/displacement vectors; most previous
neural models also allocate separate geometric tables. R032's dynamic attention
was active but unhelpful under its tested recipe. R033 rules out broad softsign
saturation,not all optimization problems. A discriminating alternative is shared
learned geometric transforms and explicitly occupancy-dependent neural messages.

This is a new parameterization/mechanism within a nonlinear relational graph,
not a claim of inventing message passing,or proof that fewer parameters are better.
Unlike additional additive pair-table conditioning,each recipient composes all
incoming learned neural messages nonlinearly;an intervening piece can transform a
fixed source/recipient message. No NNUE/Pair backbone or production change.

Prototype width8,STM-relative piece-square nodes,all ordered distinct piece pairs.
Preserve R028 material/unary prior and E[source],Q[recipient] learned vectors.
Replace the2700 independent relation vectors with a shared learned transformation
per recipient type/channel of geometric coordinates,followed by softsign:

g_ji,d=softsign(sum_k A[type_i,d,k]*x_ji,k)
h_i,d=softsign(sum(j!=i) E[v_j,d]*g_ji,d/sqrt(n-1))
logit=material/400+bias+sum unary[v_i]+sum Q[v_i,d]*h_i,d/8.

Ten fixed geometric inputs:1,df/7,dr/7,abs(df)/7,abs(dr)/7,df*dr/49,
(df^2+dr^2)/98,orthogonal alignment,diagonal alignment,knight displacement.
Two additional context inputs:aligned-and-clear line indicator,number of occupied
strictly intervening squares/7 (zero when unaligned). Endpoints excluded from
blocker count;all piece colors count. Geometry is not an attack oracle:piece type
and ownership affect learned source/recipient transforms. No pin/check labels.

GEOMETRY masks the two context inputs;CONTEXT uses them. Shared initial static
weights and zero context coefficients give exact matched initial functions.
All other E/Q/A static coefficients N(0,.1),unary/bias zero.14,209 total parameters
(E769,Q6913,A13057,12recipient types*8channels*12inputs),versus34,657 active R028
ONE parameters. Parameter-count and smoothness effects are confounded versus
R028;the matched GEOMETRY/CONTEXT contrast isolates added occupancy conditioning.
