# R003 — conditional attack/defense graph energy

Prospective hypothesis,29 October2026. Current findings motivate changing the
information basis after learning-method controls. Older F01 used mean contextual
states; W1-GATE used global softmax mixtures of local pairs. Do not repeat either.
R003 explicitly represents occupancy-dependent attack/defense edges and their
other supporting/contesting pieces. This is a discrete relational energy model,
not an NNUE hidden accumulator with renamed inputs or an NNUE correction.

Each occupied piece is a vertex. Directed geometric attacks to occupied squares
form edges,including friendly defended pieces. Sliding rays include their first
blocker; leapers,pawns,kings use ordinary geometric attacks. Pins/legal-move
restrictions are deliberately not applied; an edge is a geometric relation,not
a legal capture. Each edge table is indexed by canonical source/target piece
channels and relative displacement. CONTEXT additionally indexes clipped0/1/2
counts of other friendly defenders and enemy attackers of the target,excluding
the source. This makes third-piece occupancy central to the edge computation.
No teacher output enters inference; no tactical/material safety score is supplied.

Score in cp = fixed material + learned unary piece-square terms + bias +
sum(learned directed edge energies)/8. Three controls: UNARY,EDGE,CONTEXT. All
weights start zero; no NNUE backbone.12 piece channels,64 squares,225 displacements;
EDGE has33169 parameters,CONTEXT292369,UNARY769. Parameters are direct energies,
not dense hidden features. Context is not inherently superior; state sparsity,
symmetry,inference cost and training quality must be measured.

Initial scope: research-only full recomputation. No engine integration until
graph semantics,gradients,tiny fit,serialization/optimizer continuation and cost
pass. Full graph initially; incremental invalidation of changed rays and affected
target counts is deferred until evidence justifies that complexity. Do not claim
incremental performance from table arithmetic alone.

Feasibility: independent naive-ray/leaper oracle,canonical color/rank transform,
special-move boards,finite-difference active weights,empty/hostile inputs and
deterministic checkpoints. Match old R002 data/target split,using16k train/4096
development validation. Then8 epochs,masked Adam rate.5 cp,batch128,seeds71/97,
all three arms,sigmoid(score/400) squared loss,endpoint8 fixed before outcomes.
Before any updates, a warmed20,000-position full-inference measurement must be
<=10us/evaluation for this feasibility allocation; record and stop that arm if not.
One CPU process,one logical processor,512MiB,<=300s/arm; completed epoch checkpoint
recovery. No GPU. Feature precomputation is charged to cost and never inferred
as actual inference throughput. Small-data positive admission requires >=2%
relative validation improvement over matched EDGE in both seeds or a documented
practical trade-off; retain UNARY to distinguish relational value from unary fit.
Do not train large variants before correctness and cost screening. No playing or
architectural-success conclusion from these pilot losses.

Originality assessment is provisional: direct conditional graph energies are
computationally distinct from CP64's affine feature transform/SCReLU/readout;
competitive original research still requires learned relational utility,credible
games and practical integration. Attack features alone are not success.

## Pilot result

Independent naive geometry/context oracle and color/rank checks passed over legal
walk/special positions. Finite differences,tiny fit,CRC rejection and byte-exact
next-update continuation passed for all three modes. Initial compile's attack
table index/array confusion was repaired before any run; failed log retained.

Endpoint validation MSE (71/97): UNARY.029195/.029214,EDGE.028779/.028801,
CONTEXT.028864/.028877. CONTEXT fits training better (.025818/.025809 versus
EDGE.026501/.026485) but is slightly worse on validation in both orders. It does
not meet the2% admission rule; no larger context allocation or playing promotion.
EDGE's~1.4% unary-relative gain is small,not established strength. Full graph
inference samples EDGE.89..1.27us,CONTEXT.98..1.48us; no incremental claim and not
directly comparable to R001's different walk. All six runs completed <2s each,
with CPU-only checkpoints. Preserve r003-results.json and all raw negative results.
