## Objective and authority

Discover a self-contained, genuinely neural and substantively relational evaluator with credible
playing strength comparable to or greater than verified strong NNUE, practical
consumer-hardware training and efficient Java inference. Relations between pieces,
squares, attacks, defenses or structures must be central computational operations.
Renamed NNUE inputs, conventional NNUE with relational features, and a small NNUE
correction do not satisfy architectural success. Such controls are permissible
diagnostics. Learned transforms, quantization and conventional optimization are
allowed. No architecture is preselected. Rigorous negative closure is legitimate.

Owner priority clarification of29 October2026 is preserved in [PRIORITY](PRIORITY.md).
Primary experimental effort targets learned nonlinear relational composition,
context-dependent transformations,higher-order interactions or other justified
neural formulations. BREP2 and additive pair-table variants are controls and
evidence sources. Further pair training requires a specific consequential bounded
hypothesis serving neural BRN research; no open-ended Pair-2 optimization stream.
Additional table conditioning or additive relational inputs alone do not meet the
neural architectural objective. CP64 remains the principal strength benchmark.
Investigate failed recipes' data,capacity,regularization and optimization confounds;
do not generalize their failures to all relational architectures. Reuse existing
assets and preserve every completed result. This clarification changes priorities,
not resource protections,tournament rules,production boundaries or evidence status.

Root AGENTS, module ownership, model compatibility and Search correctness canon

## Resources and autonomous execution

Interactive Windows use, including GPU applications, takes priority. Default one
CPU experiment or active game, one search worker, BelowNormal priority, bounded
heap and no GPU allocation. Four aggregate search workers is an absolute ceiling,
not a default. Before costly work record resource estimate, observed capacity,
safe interruption/checkpoint behavior and exact restart. Reuse the existing
`nnue-control.cmd` Windows Job Object supervisor; consult actual status each root.
Its NORMAL state is necessary for supervised work but is not sufficient evidence
to monopolize CPU/GPU. Never reset a hold automatically or create a waiting daemon.

Initial CPU stages use one logical processor for JVM/BLAS work, <=2 GiB workload
memory, <=5 minutes per non-checkpointed diagnostic. Admission requires >=8 GiB
available memory and a current CPU sample below 60%; monitor long work and stop
on >=80% sustained load, <6 GiB available memory, owner hold or interactive harm.
These are conservative operational thresholds, not proof of absence of contention.
GPU work needs current evidence that its measured footprint/coexistence is safe,
or explicit owner availability confirmation; an idle sample alone is insufficient.
Larger allocations require prospective justification and safe recovery.

On resource failure stop at the earliest safe checkpoint, preserve progress,
record exact gate and next command, drain only owned processes, verify release and
end execution. Never sleep/retry availability or leave work queued. RESUME only
rechecks the recorded gate; it does not waive protections. No arbitrary token,
iteration or elapsed-time programme termination.

## Experimental method

Characterize NNUE features, math, data, objectives, optimizer, incremental state,
numerics, costs and search interaction first. Distinguish measurements, historical
claims and hypotheses. Test improved training on a relational control where
feasible before attributing an advantage to architecture. Inspect pinned Bullet
capabilities; do not assume arbitrary graph operations are supported efficiently.

Each consequential run prospectively fixes hypothesis, arms, source/model hashes,
data provenance and partitions, seed/order, objective, optimizer, exposure and
resource budget, metrics, stage gates and analysis. Preserve all failures and
negative results. Checkpoints bind weights, optimizer, cursor and config. Explicitly
record nondeterministic GPU regeneration. Use immutable output paths; compact
reports/configurations/manifests tracked, bulk evidence in ignored raw/ or build/.
Do not force-add raw data. Verify availability and integrity before reuse.

Train/validation/final evaluation are separate. Group evaluator-input aliases,
symmetries and games where identity exists; record unknown game correlations.
Already consumed panels are development only. Final data/openings must be fresh,
reserved before selection and never repeatedly optimized against. Compare matched
data/exposure and actual cost, with a bounded tuning ledger. Distillation,
search/outcome/mixed targets, curriculum and hard examples are hypotheses. No
validation-loss-only strength or architectural win.

Progress from cheap implementation/gradient/tiny-fit and resume checks, through
short controlled training, prediction/cost screening, fixed-work games, replication
and fresh confirmation. Verify board interpretation, perspective/color symmetry,
special moves, full versus incremental computation, finite/overflow behavior,
qsearch/search stability and deterministic semantics. Promote only on evidence;
diagnose failed implementations before rejecting a family. Reopen a negative
approach only with a discriminating new hypothesis.

## Playing strength and reporting

Default fixed nodes, verified at the whole-request boundary including qsearch;
fixed depth only with recorded justification/limitations. No move clocks or
clock-based tournaments without later explicit human authorization. Profiling
wall time is allowed; an administrative watchdog censors failure and cannot award
a time-forfeit win or determine a move budget. Ponder, book and tablebases off.
Matched frozen settings, paired legal openings and both colors; one active game.
Preserve complete games durably; resume skips them and repeats only unfinished
games. Caps/failures are not draws. Independent legal replay and accounting required.

Report score/WDL, opening-cluster uncertainty, caps, all-game bounds and selection
limitations. Predeclare fixed-N or valid sequential stopping; never extend to force
significance. Report equal-node, equal-depth, latency/NPS and equal-time claims
separately. Fixed nodes cannot establish practical equal-time Elo superiority.
Primary independent confirmation needs a prospectively justified precision and
either superiority (95% lower score >.5), or noninferiority (95% lower >=.45)
with a material measured cost/size/training advantage. These research criteria are
not owner acceptance. Multiple-selection/replication limitations remain explicit.

Tournament updates occur at meaningful stages or about 15 minutes, subject to
host communication requirements; no per-game narrative or acknowledgements.

human actions. Repository records must suffice without conversation history.
