gain. Test whether a single legal move away from known training positions exposes
a local generalization deficit in frozen MIX71/97 and matched LOW TIED71/97.
This diagnoses the data/objective neighborhood before another neural formulation
or training allocation. CP64 responses are reference targets, not chess truth.
No fit, games, final confirmation, Pair optimization or production change.
