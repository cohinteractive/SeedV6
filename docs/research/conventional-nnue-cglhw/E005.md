## Rationale and source

E001..E003 trained on bounded Lichess prefixes. E003 improved its enlarged teacher
cohort while remaining weak in play and regressing on the old cohort. That evidence
does not distinguish distribution, calibration, convergence and model capacity.
The existing prepared BT4/T80 cache has6,670,929,023 raw positions in16,839 verified
chunk descriptors. It supplies an established different teacher/distribution without
downloading or relabeling new data. Read-only reuse of the architecture-neutral
decoder/cache does not reactivate BRN/BRE research.

Primary evidence:
- [Publisher dataset and ODbL declaration](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xushawn/test80-bt4-relabel).
- [Publisher's relabel implementation](https://github.com/xu-shawn/lc0/commit/6ca985b81afe85f90bd79687f653f601941fa223):

  uses much larger total exposure and declining learning rate. Its1024-unit/8-output
  design is context, not evidence that copying it guarantees strength here.

Deduplicate exact placement+STM across the entire sample, validation first. Shuffle
training records with seeded Fisher-Yates. Preserve aligned raw label/unit/ordinal
provenance and distributed text/binary verification samples. Full source fingerprints
are sampled identities; do not claim full original-shard integrity verification.
Unit-disjoint validation is diagnostic; game/symmetry independence is not established.

`BT4_Q_LOGIT400_V1` inverts the publisher transform using existing Bt4Targets,
maps Q to p=(Q+1)/2, clamps p to[.0001,.9999], and stores round(400*logit(p)).
These link scores are not source CP. Bullet's sigmoid(score/400) recovers p up to
quantization/clamping. Real WDL results are retained; the first candidate uses
teacher-only loss(wdl=0) to isolate this direction. The Java validation probe's
`cp_mae` field must be interpreted as link-score MAE for this dataset.
