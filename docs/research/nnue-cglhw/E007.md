# E007 — piece-removal mechanism probe

> Historical Track A: knowledge-free NNUE versus material-bootstrapped BRN-3.
> Preserved as architectural-control evidence by the 2026-10-05 fairness amendment;
> rejection statements here do not apply to the new bootstrap-parity Track B.

Registered 2026-10-05 during E005, before these probe outcomes. Exact BRN source
proves its Gen-0 material knowledge; measure whether the tested NNUE families
nevertheless acquired consistent material-direction behavior through their random
structural initialization. This is diagnostic, not a search for favourable seeds.

Use the eight E005 families and all 32 predeclared seeds. Starting position plus
each of its 30 single non-king piece removals, correct any removed rook's castling
right, reparse FEN to regenerate keys. Both kings remain safe. These are synthetic
placements, not legal one-move transitions or claims of game reachability.
Measure each effect in White coordinates and compare its direction with the
unchanged BRN material-only reference. Do not assert that material direction is
universally the best move in arbitrary tactical positions. No probe position or
result may update a weight, select a seed or enter a Gen-0 initializer.

Report raw effects and mapped effects, ties and aggregate agreement fractions.
Expected mechanism: structurally coherent random neural representations still
lack systematic desired piece values. A contrary result is useful new evidence
and must be investigated. A positive-only material-direction constraint would
encode desired evaluation rather than a factual board relationship, and is not
authorized by the contract.

Focused placement-validity test passed. Completed all32 seeds x8 architectures
x30 removals (7,680 probes). BRN-3 agrees with the material direction in every
case, exactly as its zero-readout definition implies.
