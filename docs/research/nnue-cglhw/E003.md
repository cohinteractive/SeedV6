# E003 — local nonlinear representations and zero-readout control

> Historical Track A: knowledge-free NNUE versus material-bootstrapped BRN-3.
> Preserved as architectural-control evidence by the 2026-10-05 fairness amendment;
> rejection statements here do not apply to the new bootstrap-parity Track B.

Registered 2026-10-05, before outcomes. E002 spatial sharing substantially improves
quiet topology but each shared variant still scores 1/96 in the initial screen;
count-only numerical correction remains in progress. Test whether local nonlinear
interactions add useful Gen-0 structure while remaining incrementally maintainable.

`local3` and `local5`: shared 3x3 or 5x5 spatial kernels on 12 factual piece channels,
eight learned channels per square per perspective, ReLU at each square, sum pool
divided by sqrt(64)=8, concatenated 16 -> 32 ReLU -> 1, antisymmetric head. Only
spatial cells touching changed pieces are updated; copy their parent state, remove
old affected activations from the pool, update learned local sums, add new ones.
No full piece-pair recomputation and no handcrafted attack/material evaluation.
Parameters: local3 1,408; local5 2,944. Zero-centred Xavier-uniform weights with
fan dimensions recorded by the implementation. Float weights, double accumulators
keep pooling roundoff far below score precision. Per-ply accumulator payload:
1,040 doubles (8,320 bytes), substantially larger than HalfKP's 128 floats. Cost
and search-cache impact must be measured; parameter count alone is not efficiency.

`zero-head` control: original random HalfKP features/dense weights, zero output
weights and bias, as the neural readout portion of BRN-3 starts. It is a trainable
neural model with a constant-zero Gen-0 function, not a material evaluator. This
isolates random scoring noise; zero-head Gen-0 outcomes are seed-independent by
construction. It is not a success unless it meets the same strength/cost and
subsequent training requirements. Zeroing only the head is not pretraining.

Stage1: targeted ordinary/special/chained full-refresh checks, parent preservation,
colour and perspective symmetry, warmed six-seed cost and topology. Stage2:
local variants use the same six seeds/eight paired openings/depth3/cap2048. The
zero-head control uses seed41001 and 24 paired openings, after exact zero-function
checks across independent seeds; repeating identical games for each seed supplies
no extra initialization evidence. No trained claim, promotion or candidate seed
selection. A cost failure permits at most a labelled bounded mechanism screen.

Nine targeted tests pass across BootstrapAudit, HalfKpHead, SharedFeatures and
LocalPatchModel. Local tests include 160-ply chained update accumulation, ordinary
edges, castling, EP, promotions, exact parent retention, colour inversion and
perspective sign. Six-seed stage-1 audit now running in e003-audit.

Stage1 completed: local3 and local5 both match full-refresh outputs exactly on
all six-seed diagnostic traces. Their median update + readout costs are 1.077 us
and 1.462 us respectively; full refresh costs 2.948 us and 4.817 us. Both clearly
fit preliminary historical timing guardrails and preserve substantial incremental
advantage despite their larger per-ply state. Quiet correlation ranges .886..951
and .904..934. Zero-head gives exactly zero for all six seeds; its correlations
are undefined (zero variance), not perfect correlation. Cost 1.157 us incremental,
2.390 us full refresh. Allocation and raw timings remain in e003-audit.

Stage2 local matches and 24-pair zero-head control now running in separate JVMs.
These fixed-depth games may run concurrently; their wall times are **not** clean
NPS/performance evidence. No concurrent load was present during the cost audit.

Stage2 completed: **local3 0 wins / 0 draws / 96 losses**, **local5 1 win / 0
draws / 95 losses**, no caps. Zero-head: **0 wins / 3 draws / 45 losses** over
24 paired openings, no caps. A deterministic zero readout is therefore also far
below the .45 threshold. Reject all three as contenders. Local nonlinear context
does provide legitimate, fast incremental evaluation, but no systematic Gen-0
strength here. This rejects locality alone as the missing explanation; it does
not show trained local representations are useless. E004 tests the remaining
distinct global-interaction hypothesis with cheap low-rank updates.
