# E001 — perspective consistency crossed with score resolution

> Historical Track A: knowledge-free NNUE versus material-bootstrapped BRN-3.
> Preserved as architectural-control evidence by the 2026-10-05 fairness amendment;
> rejection statements here do not apply to the new bootstrap-parity Track B.

Registered 2026-10-05 before candidate outcomes, after baseline commit a7c5e43.

H001/H003: quiet placement representation is already smooth at fixed perspective,
but switching perspective produces inconsistent White-relative scores. Tiny
raw outputs also map to very few integer values. Distinguish these mechanisms
without changing random features, search, or adding evaluation knowledge.

Four configurations: original baseline; baseline with 32x mapping scale; odd head
`(f(us,them)-f(them,us))/2`; odd head plus 32x mapping scale. Multiplying mapping
by 32 supplies five extra bits of resolution. It is a fixed one-off diagnostic,
not a seed-specific calibration or a claim that outputs are centipawns. The .5
factor is the standard antisymmetric projection and carries no chess preference.
All use exactly the production HalfKP initializer and the same seed/parameters.
Both accumulators remain incremental. The odd head evaluates the dense layers
twice; this runtime cost may fail the frozen guardrail and must be measured.

Stage 1: six baseline seeds, same 1,280 legal edges, full/incremental oracle,
perspective consistency, quiet correlation, mapped-score vocabulary and warmed
cost. Tests require baseline wrapper equality and odd-head sign consistency with
unchanged input boards. No pretraining or corpus consumption.

Stage 2 (if correctness passes): same six seeds/eight paired openings, depth3,
cap2048. Compare raw results with the baseline; no cherry-picked seed. The
baseline wrapper gets an independent replay check. Architectures far below .45
score are mechanism evidence, not contenders for training or promotion. Retain
negative results. Even a smooth random evaluator still lacks BRN's material prior.

Stage 1 results: four targeted tests pass. Full/incremental raw error is at most
1.019e-10. Odd projection gives exact same-placement sign consistency and improves
quiet White-relative correlations to .902..939 across all six seeds. Baseline
remains -.638..097. Hires leaves raw topology unchanged as intended.

Median child-update/readout across seed medians: baseline 1.159 us, baseline-hires
1.162 us, odd 2.114 us, odd-hires 2.118 us. The odd variants are about 1.83x current
NNUE and .60x previously measured BRN-3, within the frozen screening guardrail.
They would still need co-measured BRN/independent timing confirmation if strength
justifies promotion. Same prepared trace and timing protocol as baseline.

Stage 2 completed: baseline-hires **0/0/96** W/D/L, odd **1/0/95**, odd-hires
**1/0/95**, no caps in any condition. Each uses six seeds/eight pairs/depth3/
cap2048. Raw evidence: e001-audit and e001-matches. Independent baseline-wrapper
replay passed: seed41001's 16 games match the original baseline move-for-move,
including results and nodes. Timing excluded. Evidence: e001-baseline-replay.

**Decision:** reject score-resolution-only and odd-HalfKP-only as contenders.
Do not escalate to training or claim comparability from 1/96. Retain odd-head
construction as an admissible component for representation experiments: it fixes
a real coherence defect at acceptable preliminary cost, but that mechanism alone
does not supply material knowledge. Increasing precision by 32 did not improve
aggregate strength in either head condition. Do not repeat these treatments
without a new mechanism; move to shared/factorized features and global counts.

Interpretive limit: same-placement antisymmetry is a deliberate static-model
restriction, not a theorem about chess positions with different players to move.
Tempo and zugzwang can make actual STM matter. This experiment asks whether
removing random viewpoint inconsistency helps; it does not establish that every
learned evaluator should discard all STM dependence.
