# Bootstrap evidence, 2026-10-05

> Historical Track A: knowledge-free NNUE versus material-bootstrapped BRN-3.
> Preserved as architectural-control evidence by the 2026-10-05 fairness amendment;
> rejection statements here do not apply to the new bootstrap-parity Track B.

The user's Gen-0 strength observation reproduces on this Mac. It does **not**
establish superiority of BRN-3's untrained neural representation: its zero readout
makes every fresh prediction exactly the fixed material prior. NNUE is random
with no corresponding prior. This is deliberate existing behavior, not a BRN bug.
Both are untrained, but their starting chess knowledge differs.

## Immutable reference and controls

Original source: `245a057`; production evaluator/search hashes in provenance.json.
MacBook Air arm64, Temurin 21.0.12.1+1, vector module enabled, `-Xbatch`, 1.5 GiB
maximum heap. No checkpoint/corpus reads, optimizer updates, book or tablebases.
Seeds 41001..41006 were fixed before outcomes. Opening seed 2026100502, indexed
uniform legal 6..10 opening plies, no material/evaluator filtering, both colours,
eight common openings per seed. Private 4 MiB TT per side/game, one worker,
production SearchDriver -> ExactSearchAdapter -> ExactSearch, equal required
depth, no time/node cutoff. Both neural paths have static leaves (qnodes=0), PVS,
SEE/material/quiet-history move ordering and no HCE-only selective pruning.
Production score mappings retained. No search setting favours NNUE.

BRN-3 inference: 2,368,577 float parameters (9,474,308 payload bytes); training
2,627,777 double parameters plus optimizer/scratch. Current NNUE: HalfKP
49,152 x 64, concatenated 128 -> 32 -> 1, 3,149,953 floats (12,599,812 bytes).
Thus the current gap is not evidence that NNUE simply has too few parameters.

## Mechanism diagnostics

Six seeds x 1,280 trajectory parents, generated by 16 legal-uniform games up to
80 plies using seed 2026100501. This is a diagnostic corpus, not representative
human play or a training corpus. Parent/child correlations use a common White
perspective unless labelled STM. Repeated positions remain in the sample.

- BRN readout max abs = 0; optimizer steps = 0; production and trainer predictions
  agree exactly with an independent material-only oracle at every sample.
  Distinct model hashes confirm independent internal initializations, with identical
  Gen-0 functions. Raw and quarter-residual calibration give the same fresh output.
- NNUE mapped values range -10..12 search units across seeds, versus 100 units
  per pawn for BRN. These units are not interchangeable calibrated probabilities.
- NNUE accumulator zeros 49.7..52.1%, hidden zeros 46.3..58.2%, saturation at one
  0% in both layers. This does not support upper-clipping saturation as the cause.
- Ordinary quiet moves: NNUE White-relative correlation -.638..+.097. Applying
  a null probe to the legal child where not in check, to hold evaluator STM fixed,
  yields .892..945. Perspective inconsistency therefore confounds a naive claim
  that the placement representation is intrinsically discontinuous.
- King moves at fixed STM: .146..603; captures .823..913. King-index row changes
  remain a separately motivated representation issue.
- Same-placement legal null probes: NNUE White-relative correlation -.625..+.110.
  BRN is unchanged in White coordinates by construction.
- Colour swap plus rank reflection preserves both evaluators (NNUE float-order
  error below displayed precision). File reflection on the 676 samples without
  castling rights yields NNUE correlation -.171..+.095, versus BRN 1.0.
- BRN quiet-move distance is exactly zero; capture mean absolute change is 2.664
  pawns. This topology is entirely that of fixed material, not the relational net.
- Full-refresh vs one-step incremental NNUE raw error <= 1.019e-10. Existing
  accumulator tests additionally cover special moves and correctness boundaries.

No piece-removal synthetic legality corpus, spatial-distance stratification,
PV/cutoff study, branch-counter measurement or trained topology analysis has run
yet. Those are hypothesis-driven extensions, not implied by these measurements.

## Reproduction and validation

Output directories must not already exist. The tool refuses to overwrite evidence.

```sh
./gradlew :app:test -Pheadless --tests '*BootstrapAuditTest' --tests '*Brn3SearchCalibrationTest' --tests '*NnueAccumulatorTest' --tests '*NnueNetworkTest' --console=plain
./gradlew :app:nnueBootstrapAudit -PauditOutput=/tmp/seed-audit-new -PauditArgs='audit 41001,41002,41003,41004,41005,41006 8 3 2048'
./gradlew :app:nnueBootstrapAudit -PauditOutput=/tmp/seed-match-new -PauditArgs='match 41001,41002,41003,41004,41005,41006 8 3 2048'
python3 tools/analyze-nnue-bootstrap.py > /tmp/seed-summary.json
```

Build passed. Targeted tests: 23 passed, 0 failed/skipped (3 bootstrap/symmetry,
2 BRN calibration, 14 NNUE accumulator, 4 NNUE initialization). Reproduction of
16 full games passed. Broad suite, training, million-position comparison,
packaging and GUI not run: no candidate or production behavior change exists yet.
The harness uses existing persistence encoders only for reproducible model hashes.
No application weights, Best stores, BRN semantics or NNUE baseline were modified.

Audit and topology directories retain per-seed metrics, model hashes, environment
and corpus FENs; depth directories retain every opening and move. summary.json is
derived by tools/analyze-nnue-bootstrap.py. Baseline outcomes and source provenance
remain available even after the tool evolves. Finalizer was not invoked, per the
owner's explicit workstream instruction. No version bump is claimed.

## Decision

Continue with original BRN-3 as the deliberately knowledge-advantaged practical
reference. Do not attribute its Gen-0 advantage to random relational topology.
Investigate NNUE perspective consistency, representation sharing and quantization
under the newly frozen gates. No success, impossibility, in-scope exhaustion or
fairness-boundary terminal outcome has been established. Required human action:
None; optional reference clarification can redirect future work.
