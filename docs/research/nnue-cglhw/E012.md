## Identity and backward compatibility decision

Choose Option B. Two persisted identities are required because optimizer objectives
differ. `NNUE` retains its historical wire schema, bytes, initializer, objective,
and campaign binding; human-facing selectors call it **NNUE (legacy, no material)**.
`NNUE_MATERIAL`, displayed **NNUE (material parity)**, is the default for new Arena
campaigns and has a distinct schema, model magic, optimizer magic, filename, and
recipe. Model and optimizer reads reject the other identity. No automatic migration,
reinterpretation of trained weights, or modification of the old campaign occurs.

The new model deliberately matches E008: same HalfKP geometry, seed initializer,
V1 neural mapping and integer material addition. It does not substitute the E009
zero-head, pawn-residual, masked-Adam research model. E008/E010/E011 Gen-0 evidence
therefore applies to its evaluator; trained strength still requires measurement.

Let `u` be the learned raw output, `v=tanh(u)`, `S=32511`, and `M` the verified
STM material in integer engine units (P100,N320,B330,R500,Q900,K0). Search computes
`clip(M + V1.map(v), -S, S)`. Training and held-out validation use its continuous
counterpart `y=clip(v + M/S, -1, 1)` and existing half-squared outcome loss.
Only the neural contribution is trainable; its derivative is
`(y-target)*(1-v*v)` inside the clamp and zero at/outside saturation. Target labels
and Adam hyperparameters remain unchanged. Search quantization differs from the
continuous objective by at most one engine unit. This preserves the measured
uncalibrated neural scale; it does not claim the neural output is centipawns or
adopt BRN-3's distinct CE objective or .25 residual gain. Material is present
through training, validation, checkpoint resume, self-play and ordinary search.

The pure fixed-material formula, incremental delta and clipping are unchanged.
Earlier knowledge-free architectural controls remain valid. E009 remains a
separately identified, prospective equal-objective training experiment.

positions reconstructed from the real campaign. These small results validate the
software integration; they do not establish a new statistical strength claim.
