## Programme challenge and implementation sequence

The flattened main/q kernel admits only Policy 4 and unpruned controls. Existing
recursive research factories are mutually guarded; removing guards would silently
enable policies the flat continuation machine does not execute. Rich composition
therefore needs explicit shared-core integration, with research-owned activation.
Avoid a second engine, generic dispatch framework, or broad rewrite. The existing
AspirationSearch is single-worker, whereas ROOT_REUSE now has a verified narrow
window seam from Workstream 2. Use it and account for every retry/helper.

1. Immutable composition, independent controls, configuration identity, parallel
   aspiration; retain baseline exact/reference and production-equivalence tests.
2. Combined ordering and truthful history learning, followed by bounded depth
   management and calibrated selective consumers in the flat continuation machine.
   Validate retries, original windows, credits, real ply and conservative proof
   suppression as each group enters. Keep generated legal lists and searched
   prefixes intact; omitted moves create no learning labels.
3. Review all selected subjects against actual composition and alternatives;
   record defensible exclusions instead of forcing incompatible mechanisms.
4. Freeze candidate/configurations and fresh real-model inputs. Run independent
   semantic, integration, lifecycle, worker and whole-request resource diagnostics.
   Construction needs no independent Elo proof or new champion tournament.

Detailed inherited evidence remains in existing SR reports. Check raw evidence
availability before depending on numerical claims. New raw output belongs under
`p3-construction-2026-10-20/raw/`; compact protocols, configurations, manifests
and summaries remain tracked. Never extend frozen predecessor experiments.

## Verified groups 2-3: ordering, depth and selective composition

Group 2 combines capture history, immediate continuation history, two killers,
countermoves and nominal-only main-history learning. All are individually
controllable and worker-private. Nominal observations refer to the assigned child
depth, not exact proof through selective descendants. Reduced unverified failures
and omitted moves supply no nominal malus. Rich tactical keys combine immediate
exchange material with bounded capture history; good tacticals precede quiet
refutations/history, with bad tacticals optionally last. That variant generates
quiets before sorting the complete list; turning it off retains staged generation.
It does not generate or search a legal move twice. The independent unpruned
comparisons, removal controls and learning/reset tests pass (`group2-tests.json`).

Group 3 uses raw same-side two-ply evaluation movement for one-ply LMR relief,
and node-local CAUTION relief after an optimistic reduction fails required nominal
verification. Both retain subsequent original-window PVS re-search. A shared
one-ply path cap combines forced checked evasions at remaining depth <=2 and
nonchecking recaptures at the depth-one boundary. Credits and assigned child depth
survive retries, real ply still controls mates, and no extension policy consumes
or publishes ordinary TT proof. Completed legal guidance remains separate.

New shallow Pair-2 hypotheses are reverse futility at D1/D2 with margin **200*d**,
and quiet futility after three searched quiets with margin **100*d**. These are
explicit configurable quarter-residual score units, not transplanted HCE margins
or calibrated WDL claims. Both protect checked, sparse, rule-clock and mate domains;
quiet futility additionally protects pawn/king moves, checking children and legal
hash guidance. Positive main history can protect WINDOW/futility candidates.
Raw evaluation is cached only within the current real-path node and remains
distinct from predictions and TT proof. Every omission predicts a threshold,
retains a searched PV prefix, and conservatively taints ancestors. The initial
configuration enables all 18 implemented controls; economical fresh diagnostics
must still characterize its incidence, disagreement and costs before closeout.
