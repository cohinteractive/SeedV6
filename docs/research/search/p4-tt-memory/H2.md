# H2: bounded score age with independent completed-hint freshness

Status: selected hypothesis, not yet an executable candidate. Subordinate to
[TT-R001](CONTRACT.md) and Search P4-R001. H1 remains frozen at a72cc496/build81;
build80 is the technical control and build79 the accepted champion.

The [H1 lifetime diagnostic](h1-lifetime-v1-summary.json) measured 104 consecutive
real-history requests per W1/W4 domain. Stale entries supplied 11.96%/11.10% of
score hits; 98.90%/98.93% of those stale hits were exactly one request old. The
sum of per-request unique stale nonroot entries was 63,295/59,620, including
5,739/4,989 at nominal depth at least three. Stale same-key refreshes replaced a
deeper prior score with a shallower fresh score 15,874/14,865 times. No score-to-
hint-only demotion occurred in this corpus. W1 instrumentation matched clean,
persistent H1 on every request. W4 scheduling neutrality is not claimed.

These are availability and replacement measurements, not validated cutoff
opportunities or strength. The observer did not see requested depth/window or
remaining extension credit. The scope is eight actor streams from four existing
development games, positions before plies 16-41 and 100,000-node requests.

## Integrated candidate to implement and qualify

- Add an explicit research feature dependent on HEURISTIC_TT_MEMORY. Preserve
  normal production selection and H1 selection. Retain CP64, all original18
  controls, verified-null accounting, both extensions/cap, ADAPTIVE16, ROOT_REUSE,
  history-rich keys and coherent synchronization.
- Permit ordinary heuristic scores from the current or immediately preceding
  request, retaining all H1 legality, sufficient-depth, interior-null-window,
  normal-score/window, non-synthetic and non-verifier restrictions. Every reuse
  remains selective provenance. No root/PV-window/mate shortcut or proof claim.
- For the same key, extend H1's existing two-ply refresh slack to a score one
  request old, so a shallow new iteration need not erase a deeper prior estimate.
  For a collision, discount a prior-request score's protected depth by two plies;
  current completed guidance retains its own depth protection. Equal priority
  admits the newer completion. These are declared hypotheses, not optimal tuning.
- Refresh a completed legal hint independently of score freshness. A current
  deeper hint survives shallower writes even if its retained score is older.
  A hint cannot make an old score younger. Expired scores may still supply legal
  guidance, but cannot trigger a cutoff. Ordinary proof entries remain protected.
- Preserve the three-array, direct-mapped capacity and payload. The planned
  representation uses the existing heuristic/hint marker combination to identify
  an aged record, plus the three spare low-word bits for hint generation modulo8.
  Treat that short hint generation as current only while score age is at most1;
  this guard and the existing full-table generation-wrap clear prevent aliasing.
  Unmarked H1 records retain their existing interpretation. Reject the design if
  coherent independent freshness cannot be demonstrated; do not silently add a
  new layout or relax age checks.
- A newly completed mate-domain outcome must discard a same-key retained
  ordinary heuristic score while preserving legal guidance. Never store or reuse
  heuristic mate scores. Keep raw-evaluation tables untouched.

The one-request age cap follows the measured concentration of stale encounters;
it does not imply older scores are universally useless. Separate hint freshness
is a necessary retention dependency, not a demand for independently winning parts.
No clustered table, qTT, correction history or other Search subsystem is selected.

## Qualification and next decision

Before games, exercise depth/age/bound admission, independent hint survival,
collision priority, expiry, modulo8 and full-generation wrap, ordinary/raw-eval
separation, mate-domain invalidation and coherent concurrent access. Verify actual
cross-request cutoffs, selective provenance, legal/history/board restoration,
synthetic/null-verifier exclusion, W1-16 limits/cancel/drain/reuse, unchanged
independent exact controls, and normal/H1 routing. Run focused and proportionate
broader regressions, then freeze a distinct source/build/executable identity.

Choose controlled playing evidence only after qualification. Compare against
unchanged-policy build80 and retain H1 as an explicit predecessor for causal
diagnostics or reverse ablation where useful. Champion79 comparisons remain
required before a champion-relative claim. H1 attempt1 is still operationally
incomplete and its nominal alpha0.025 is spent; a later confirmation uses a new
prospective allocation and independent inputs. No acceptance or promotion follows
from implementing H2 or from an efficiency measurement.
