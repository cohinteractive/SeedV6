Technical implementation and information-use diagnostic, 30 October 2026.
W1 strength screen is **promising but inconclusive**; W4 screen is active.
Build79 remains the champion; unchanged-policy build80
is the causal technical control. H1 is never the production default.

H1 adds HEURISTIC_TT_MEMORY to the CP64 production composition: flags7602175,
otherwise identical original18/NOMINAL_FUTILITY/VERIFIED_NULL, margins and cap,
ADAPTIVE16 and OWNER/ROOT_REUSE. An explicit research UCI launcher applies the
existing BOUNDARY2 allocation AND wrapper; normal UCI/GUI/Arena routing is unchanged.

The existing 24-byte direct-mapped slot stores a separate heuristic-domain marker
and a completed-move depth alongside score depth. Ordinary TT probes cannot read
heuristic scores. Raw-evaluation tables remain separate and unchanged. Score
labels classify outcomes against the producing window; inside-window is a
selective estimate, not exhaustive minimax. Every reused score taints invocation
provenance. No heuristic mate score is stored or consumed; ordinary values need no
ply normalization. Terminal/rule checks precede probing.

Eligibility: current generation (one whole driver request), sufficient nominal
depth, interior null window, ordinary score/window, validated legal move, no
synthetic pass or legal null-verification subtree. Root/restricted-root cutoffs,
PV-window shortcuts, qTT and leaf TT are excluded. History-rich SearchKey and
synchronized stripe publication remain unchanged. Subtree completion and checkpoint
requirements remain; abandoned ancestors do not publish completed estimates.

Retention: current-generation deeper collision information survives. Same-key
score refresh permits up to two plies of depth loss; this is a declared exploratory
setting, not evidence-based optimal tuning. Completed move and score depths are
independent: deeper hints survive shallow score writes, and later completed hints
can refresh while retaining a score. A new-generation hint cannot extend the old
score's eligibility. This configuration is integrated; its pieces need not win
independently. Capacity is still 2,097,152 slots / 50,331,648 entry payload bytes at
Hash64; headers, locks, heap and process memory are distinct quantities.
