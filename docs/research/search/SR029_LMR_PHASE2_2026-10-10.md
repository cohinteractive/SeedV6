   ordinal >=8, R=3 at depth >=8 and ordinal >=16. Positive reduced child depth.

   parent's later moves; a materially different PV/resource protection hypothesis.

numeric margin, SEE pruning, root/tactical reduction or speculative node-role
classification. Nested reductions are explicit selective work; every reduced
alpha raise (including beta exceedance), and every mate-band reduced return,

inside-window improvement. Nominal verification does not erase other selective
dependencies. Conservatively monotone epoch suppression may retain more taint
than necessary; it makes no unproved dependency-discharge claim. Reduced fail-low
predicts at most the captured alpha, never a nominal mathematical fail-soft bound.
Ordinary TT applicability and storage architecture remain unchanged.

Actual searched ordinal counts each distinct begun legal child once, including
earlier tactical stages, excluding repeated scouts/re-search. First legal move
keeps its full window. Real ply, repetition path and one child preparation remain
stable through retries. Existing qsearch proof suppression stays intact.

Accounting challenge: the current adapter classifies qnodes by real ply versus
root nominal depth. With reductions that would misclassify early qsearch.
Use actual child search domain for successful admission accounting; preserve
whole-request totals and aborted-work budgets. Optional research instrumentation
