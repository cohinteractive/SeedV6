## Stable objective and acceptance

Develop and technically qualify an externally informed TT information architecture
that enables representative selective Search consumers to exploit useful information.
Investigate production, meaning/provenance, identity, admission, retention, replacement,
lifetime, eligibility and consumption together. An implemented but unused capability,
more storage or more hits alone cannot qualify. Complexity needs demonstrated purpose.

Successful foundational qualification requires all of:

A. A characterized real information-flow limitation or architectural dependency.
B. Truthful production, preservation and exposure of useful information in selective Search.
C. Consequential downstream consumption under realistic Search conditions.
D. A meaningful observable Search consequence beyond storage or API/hit counters.
E. Characterized memory, runtime, Search effort, quality, concurrency and lifecycle costs.
F. Protected chess correctness, score semantics, state/history, reliability and reporting.
G. Reproducible formulation, implementation/configuration, evidence and limitations.

Immediate standalone Elo superiority is not mandatory. The practical connection to
stronger Search must be credible and supported, adverse evidence investigated, and
unacceptable costs without supported downstream benefit prevent qualification.
Technical qualification is a proposal for GPT/owner reconciliation and acceptance;
it is not champion advancement, production selection or confirmed playing strength.
The owner retains authority over material changes to this objective, scope and criteria.
