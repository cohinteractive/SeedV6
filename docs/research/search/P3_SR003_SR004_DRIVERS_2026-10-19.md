## Bootstrap challenge

Historical MtdResearchSearch is a static-leaf HCE single-worker control, not an
integrated candidate. Its PREV/ORACLE/two-pass/directional-bracket variants,
integer-domain safeguards and cancellation tests are useful references. Its
negative measurements do not reject current integrated MTD(f).

Current TTable is direct mapped, coherently striped, requested 64 MiB, with
equal-depth/current-generation/cutoff-only proof and unchanged replacement.
Same-depth same-type bounds cannot replace an existing ordinary entry, even
when stronger; no dual-bound storage, qTT or non-cutting tightening is permitted.
Nominal qsearch and selective work suppress ordinary proof. Consequently a
selective zero-window result cannot acquire proof authority by joining a driver
interval. A final searched interior result/PV is required; inconsistent estimates
must trigger a full-window fallback, never an assertion of exact convergence.

IterationReuse currently accepts only full-window completed invocations. Narrow
passes clear its previous guidance. Repeated history resets and lost guidance are
real integration costs; assess them explicitly before claiming an isolated driver
comparison. ROOT_REUSE coordinates restricted eldest/scout/full jobs and drains
all helpers; merely applying a single-worker wrapper does not implement parallel
MTD. Binding proof, restricted-root and completion rules remain unchanged.

External hypothesis: [Plaat's own MTD(f) description](https://askeplaat.wordpress.com/534-2/mtdf-algorithm/)
emphasizes first guesses and TT memory to avoid repeated work. This motivates
measurement; it establishes no SeedV6 performance fact.

## Interpretation boundaries and retained alternatives

The cost comparison holds kernel, evaluator/mapping, selectivity flags, TT size,
replacement/proof rules, history lifetime, worker count, request limits and JVM
settings constant. Root-window changes necessarily alter WINDOW/LMR eligibility,
ordering and invocation resets. Ordinary narrow variants also lose completed-root
guidance under the existing full-window-only eligibility rule; GUARDED_TWO tests
the bounded remedy without accepting speculative guidance. These are integrated
configuration comparisons, not isolated causal estimates of only window width.
Selective score/PV differences are preserved and are not exact-mode failures.
Independent unpruned references passed; no oracle was modified to match a candidate.

The TT alternatives are explicitly **unimplemented, owner-reserved hypotheses**:
retaining stronger same-type or dual bounds might help exact-tree retries, but the
observed scarcity of ordinary producers makes that alone an unlikely remedy here.
A qsearch/qualified-selective cache could reduce re-expansion, but would need a
separate justified identity and proof/prediction domain, including window-dependent
omissions, ordering/history dependencies, legal PV recovery, mate normalization,
replacement, allocation and coherent concurrency. A selective estimate is not an
ordinary proof even if cached twice. Neither predicted benefit is verified, and no
policy change is necessary merely to conclude this bounded comparison. No such
change or dependent implementation is authorized by these observations.

All six initial policies and GUARDED_TWO remain executable research comparisons
over the shared kernel. Historical static HCE MTD/ORACLE controls remain intact.
There is no separate MTD Search kernel, second production engine, production
default change, evaluator-family substitution or feature-rich construction.
The parallel seam accepts narrow windows only for ROOT_REUSE and preserves the
existing full-window route for other coordinator policies. Exact mandatory
reduced-result verification, restricted-root proof exclusion, invocation-local
history, real-history restoration and helper drainage remain core-owned.
