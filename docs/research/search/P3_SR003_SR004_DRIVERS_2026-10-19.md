## Bootstrap challenge

Historical MtdResearchSearch is a static-leaf HCE single-worker control, not an
integrated candidate. Its PREV/ORACLE/two-pass/directional-bracket variants,
integer-domain safeguards and cancellation tests are useful references. Its
negative measurements do not reject current integrated MTD(f).

Current TTable is direct mapped, coherently striped, requested 64 MiB, with
equal-depth/current-generation/cutoff-only proof and unchanged replacement.
Same-depth same-type bounds cannot replace an existing ordinary entry, even
when stronger; no dual-bound storage, qTT or non-cutting tightening is permitted.
Nominal qsearch and selective work suppress ordinary proof. Consequently a
selective zero-window result cannot acquire proof authority by joining a driver
interval. A final searched interior result/PV is required; inconsistent estimates
must trigger a full-window fallback, never an assertion of exact convergence.

IterationReuse currently accepts only full-window completed invocations. Narrow
passes clear its previous guidance. Repeated history resets and lost guidance are
real integration costs; assess them explicitly before claiming an isolated driver
comparison. ROOT_REUSE coordinates restricted eldest/scout/full jobs and drains
all helpers; merely applying a single-worker wrapper does not implement parallel
MTD. Binding proof, restricted-root and completion rules remain unchanged.

External hypothesis: [Plaat's own MTD(f) description](https://askeplaat.wordpress.com/534-2/mtdf-algorithm/)
emphasizes first guesses and TT memory to avoid repeated work. This motivates
measurement; it establishes no SeedV6 performance fact.
