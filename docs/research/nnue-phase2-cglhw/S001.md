# S001 — prospective fixed paired strength screening

Frozen before Phase2 games. Comparator is exact bundled WDL25, SHA256
a25e76fcdfe1b854452685bec87bc759bed2ed166312579e024410f0903517ab.
CP64 is historical only unless a specifically justified reproduction control is
later frozen. All primary matches use identical explicit CLV_C1/BOUNDARY2,
Windows Java21, W1/Hash32, Vector API, one active game, no book/tablebase/ponder,
200ms/move,600-ply cap censored, formal python-chess outcomes and no score
adjudication. Both engines use the same frozen research runtime and -Xbatch.

Select32 fresh paired legal16-ply roots with a seeded label-blind shuffle, excluding
tracked prior roots including Phase1 screen/confirmation. Reserve separate256
confirmation and128 optional robustness roots before play. These partitions are
disjoint positions, not a claim of independent source games/opening families or
disjointness from all training positions. Freeze exact source/hash/seed and complete
selection manifest. No post-result resampling.

Every technically valid initial A001/B001/B002 candidate gets64 games on the same
32 development roots, unless a documented correctness, inadequate-training or
measured impractical-cost finding excludes it. All64 games count; no score-based
early stop. Same roots permit paired descriptive contrasts but create selection
dependence: screen estimates and intervals are exploratory. Small differences or
intervals spanning zero are inconclusive, not equivalence. Negative results remain.

Before any games, add K001: the exact WDL25 payload in the experimental envelope
with the corrected bounded-integer SIMD kernel. Exact259-position parity and zero
hot allocation pass; same-harness update+head cost is approximately675ns versus
743ns for production. This control separates an implementation-efficiency change
from learned architecture/training changes. It also receives64 paired games if
the budget permits; fixed-work search equivalence/cost is checked first. All eight
initial screen candidates remain exploratory, with a separate independent winner
confirmation required for a positive strength recommendation.

Warmup is two passes of the same12 fixed diagnostic roots at20000 nodes per root
per engine, outside scored play; distinct game identities reset TT. Record every
warmup request and validate legal moves. Node-based warmup is a runtime preparation
step, not equal-node strength evidence. Match budgeting includes observed warmup.
Failures preserve partial logs, never produce a scored win/loss by convenience.

Per-screen initial wall cap2400s; initial aggregate screen ceiling14400s. Reassess
economics before launching any screen that cannot fit the remaining allowance.
No training/build/benchmark runs concurrently with timed games. Fixed-endpoint
analysis independently replays all PGNs, verifies both colours/history, actual
move logs, model/runtime identities, terminations, cap status and paired intervals.
No interim significance testing or candidate training changes during a match.

A candidate with credible positive screen evidence and acceptable inference/search
economics can earn a separately frozen independent confirmation. A promising
architecture with an identified correctable training/cost limitation can earn one
specific revision, with a fresh prospective recipe/endpoint. Small screens cannot
conclusively reject small gains. A bad model does not by itself reject a family.

Independent confirmation has NOT been selected or launched. Candidate hash, sample,
family size, practical threshold, lower-bound gates, clocks and budget must be
frozen before any reserved games. No repeated retest of the same failed endpoint.
Longer-clock/multiworker robustness requires separately stated applicability and
does not follow automatically from a short-clock success.

[Fishtest's statistical documentation](https://official-stockfish.github.io/docs/fishtest-wiki/Fishtest-Mathematics.html)
models paired-game totals rather than treating opposite-colour results as
independent observations. This programme retains existing fixed-endpoint paired
bootstrap/normal analysis; it does not claim to implement Fishtest's GSPRT or
inherit its error guarantees. The [official FAQ](https://official-stockfish.github.io/docs/fishtest-wiki/Fishtest-FAQ.html)
also emphasizes common outcome distributions across workers and staged testing.
Here the hardware/runtime is controlled locally, development is separated from
confirmation, and repeated attempts require a prospective family allowance.
